# qwen/qwen3.6-35b-a3b

_(158.7s, 1629 chars)_

**Sequencing, Filtering, and Normalization**

Initial processing encompassed 84 samples, yielding 1,398,204 raw reads. Following quality filtering, 519,769 reads were retained (37.2% retention rate). After prokaryotic subset extraction and sequence normalization, 11 samples (SRR38958117–SRR38958158) met the downstream retention criteria, comprising 46,656 reads and 161 amplicon sequence variants (ASVs). Chloroplast-derived sequences were minimal, consisting of a single ASV detected across three samples (26 reads). The complete filtering and normalization workflow is summarized in Figure 1.

**Taxonomic Resolution and Community Composition**

Taxonomic assignment was performed against the SILVA database. Of the 162 sequences successfully assigned taxonomy, 162 were resolved to the domain, phylum, and class levels, 161 to the order level, 158 to the family level, and 137 to the genus level. At the phylum level, bacterial communities across the 11 normalized samples were predominantly composed of Pseudomonadota (116 ASVs), followed by Bacteroidota (22 ASVs), Campylobacterota (13 ASVs), Verrucomicrobiota (4 ASVs), and Actinomycetota (3 ASVs) (Table 1). Genus-level resolution was achieved for 84.6% of classified sequences (137/162), with the remaining classified sequences restricted to higher taxonomic ranks.
