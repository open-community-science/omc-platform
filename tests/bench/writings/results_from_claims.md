# Results from claims (qwen/qwen3.6-35b-a3b)

_10/12 verified · 8 computations · 4 unsupported numbers_

Initial sequencing of the frost flower samples from the Ice Chamber Experiment Metagenome (PRJNA1473294) yielded 1,398,204 raw reads across 84 samples. Following primer removal, 860,222 reads remained. After quality filtering, 519,769 reads were retained, corresponding to a 37.2% retention rate. It should be noted that quality filtering resulted in the loss of 73 of the 84 initial samples, with 11 samples retained for downstream analysis. The final denoised dataset comprised 161 prokaryotic amplicon sequence variants (ASVs), with a total of 46,656 reads assigned to these ASVs across the 11 retained samples. Taxonomic classification against the SILVA database yielded 162 ASVs classified at the domain level, 162 at the phylum level, 162 at the class level, 161 at the order level, 158 at the family level, and 137 at the genus level. Pseudomonadota was the dominant phylum with 116 ASVs, followed by Bacteroidota (22 ASVs), Campylobacterota (13 ASVs), Verrucomicrobiota (4 ASVs), and Actinomycetota (3 ASVs). PCA ordination of relative abundance data showed PC1 explained 43.1% and PC2 explained 33.8% of variance (Figure 1). Bray-Curtis dissimilarity between the most similar pair (SRR38958117 and SRR38958158) was 0.1746, while the most dissimilar pair (SRR38958135 and SRR38958147) was 0.9372 (Table 1).
