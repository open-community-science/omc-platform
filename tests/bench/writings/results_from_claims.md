# Results from claims (qwen/qwen3.6-35b-a3b)

_10/10 claims verified; 0 unsupported numbers_

**Results**

Of the 84 initial samples, only 11 yielded prokaryotic ASVs after filtering and normalization. Sequencing retention was 37.2%, with 519,769 reads retained from 1,398,204 input reads. The 11 retained samples contributed a total of 46,656 prokaryotic reads after normalization. A total of 161 prokaryotic ASVs were recovered across the 11 retained samples. One chloroplast ASV was detected across 3 samples with 26 reads (Table 1). Taxonomic classification was performed against the SILVA database. Pseudomonadota was the dominant phylum, accounting for 116 of 162 classified ASVs. Bacteroidota was the second most abundant phylum with 22 classified ASVs. Campylobacterota comprised 13 of the classified ASVs, ranking third among phyla (Figure 1). Of the 162 total ASVs, 137 were classified to the genus rank.
