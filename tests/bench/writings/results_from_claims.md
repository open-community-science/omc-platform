# Results from claims (qwen/qwen3.6-35b-a3b)

_16/18 verified · 30 computations · 1 unsupported numbers_

For the 16S amplicon sequencing of frost flowers from the Ice Chamber Experiment Metagenome (PRJNA1473294) on an Illumina MiSeq platform, the initial dataset comprised 84 samples containing 1,398,204 raw reads. Following primer removal, 860,222 reads remained. After quality filtering, 519,769 reads were retained, representing 37.2% of the raw reads. Subsequent denoising and renormalization reduced the dataset from 84 samples to 11 retained samples. Across these 11 samples, 161 prokaryotic ASVs were retained, yielding a total of 46,656 reads. A total of 162 ASVs were classified against the SILVA database. Taxonomic classification was achieved for 162/162 ASVs at the domain and phylum levels, 161/162 at the class level, 161/162 at the order level, 158/162 at the family level, and 137/162 at the genus level (Table 1). At the phylum level, the ASVs were distributed as Pseudomonadota (116 ASVs), Bacteroidota (22), Campylobacterota (13), Verrucomicrobiota (4), and Actinomycetota (3).

Shannon diversity (bits) per sample ranged from 2.01 to 4.71, with a mean of 3.82. Observed richness ranged from 19 to 98 ASVs per sample, with a mean of 58.73. Sample SRR38958118 exhibited the highest Shannon diversity (4.71 bits) and highest observed richness (98 ASVs), whereas SRR38958147 displayed the lowest Shannon diversity (2.01 bits) and lowest observed richness (32 ASVs). Total reads per sample varied from 1,218 (SRR38958128) to 7,026 (SRR38958118). The mean pairwise Bray-Curtis dissimilarity between samples was 0.635 (Figure 1).

Regarding data quality, the retention of only 37.2% of raw reads after filtering indicates aggressive quality filtering that may have removed valid biological signal. Additionally, one chloroplast ASV was detected across three samples with 26 total reads, indicating potential mitochondrial or chloroplast contamination. These observations are reported to ensure transparent documentation of the dataset characteristics.
