# Results

## Sequence Processing and ASV Generation

A total of 1,398,204 reads were obtained from 11 samples. Following the filtering process, 519,769 reads (37.2% of the input) were retained. After renormalization, the final dataset consisted of 161 prokaryotic ASVs across the 11 samples, totaling 46,656 reads. Additionally, one chloroplast ASV was identified in 3 samples, accounting for 26 reads.

## Taxonomic Composition

Taxonomic classification was performed using the Silva database. A total of 162 ASVs were classified at the domain, phylum, and class levels. Classification was achieved at the order level for 161 ASVs, the family level for 158 ASVs, and the genus level for 137 ASVs [Table 1]. 

The taxonomic distribution at the phylum level was characterized by the presence of five primary groups: Pseudomonadota (116 ASVs), Bacteroidota (22 ASVs), Campylobacterota (13 ASVs), Verrucomicrobiota (4 ASVs), and Actinomycetota (3 ASVs) [Figure 1].
