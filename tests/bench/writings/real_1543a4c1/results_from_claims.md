# Results from claims (qwen/qwen3.6-35b-a3b)

_10/12 verified · 9 computations · 0 unsupported numbers_

Initial sequencing of the frost flower samples from the Ice Chamber Experiment Metagenome (PRJNA1473294) yielded 84 samples comprising 1,398,204 raw reads. Following quality filtering, the read retention rate was 67.9%. After denoising and chimera removal, 44 samples remained for prokaryote analysis, yielding 207,607 reads and 414 amplicon sequence variants (ASVs). A quality caveat is noted: 40 samples were lost during the pipeline (84 initial → 44 retained for prokaryote analysis).

Across all domains, a total of 735 ASVs were classified against the SILVA database. The most abundant phyla by ASV count were Pseudomonadota (320 ASVs), SAR (176), Amorphea (77), Bacteroidota (51), and Archaeplastida (42). Taxonomic classification completeness varied by rank, with assignments achieved for domain (735/735), phylum (715/735), class (719/735), order (710/735), family (652/735), and genus (537/735).

Within the 44 retained prokaryote samples, library sizes ranged from 1,110 to 20,267 reads. The mean Shannon diversity was 3.9973 (range: 1.10–5.33 bits), and the mean observed richness was 71.48 ASVs (range: 16–153).
