Submit Data to ENA

Prepare and submit metagenomic sequence data to the European Nucleotide Archive. You bring your own Webin credentials — OMC provides an AI assistant and metadata editor to help you through the process.

How it works

  1. Launch a session

    An isolated container with an AI chat assistant and a Marimo metadata editor

  2. Prepare your metadata

    The AI helps you pick the right ENA checklist and fill in required fields in the Notebook tab

  3. Upload your files

    FTP your sequence files to ENA directly — the assistant generates the commands for you

  4. Submit

    Register your study, samples, experiments, and runs through the portal's ENA proxy

Start an ENA Session

The session gives you:

  • Chat — AI assistant for ENA metadata questions, checklist selection, ENVO ontology guidance
  • Notebook — Marimo dataframe editor pre-populated with the right columns for your checklist
  • Workspace — writable directory for exporting TSV files and study metadata

You'll need:

  • An ENA Webin account — register here if you don't have one
  • Your sequence files ready for FTP upload
  • MD5 checksums for each file
